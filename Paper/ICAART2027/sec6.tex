\section{\uppercase{Conclusion}} \label{sec6}
Offline objective-specific ranking supports preference-conditioned selection among heterogeneous RAG configurations. In this exploratory evaluation, Balanced performs close to the strong fixed reference, while comparisons with Adaptive-RAG are conditional on our GraphRAG-based routing implementation. Feature ablation establishes a modest ranking gain, not a final QA advantage over configuration-only ranking. Future work requires an independent test set, downstream comparison with configuration-only rankers and strong fixed settings, measurement of document acquisition/preparation and feature-extraction costs, and reference-free shortlist selection. Further work will investigate higher-order document representations, including graph connectivity, and automatic priority estimation from question difficulty and runtime constraints. These extensions require evaluation against strong fixed settings rather than assuming that additional adaptation improves final QA.
